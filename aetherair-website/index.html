The Challenge

Design an airline website that feels calm and immersive without sacrificing clarity or usability. Most airline websites prioritize speed and density, often overwhelming users with information. The challenge was to create a structure that supports storytelling while still guiding users efficiently through key content.

The Solution

I designed a modular, scroll-based website that uses motion and spacing to guide attention rather than overwhelm it. Subtle scroll-trigger animations, restrained transitions, and a clear content hierarchy allow users to explore the brand at a relaxed pace while maintaining intuitive navigation.

My Approach

Scroll as Narrative

The site is structured as a vertical journey, with each section designed as a moment rather than a page.

Motion with Intent

Scroll-trigger animations guide attention through gradual reveals and soft transitions, supporting focus without distraction.

Clarity Over Complexity

Spacing, hierarchy, and restrained interaction design keep the experience calm, intuitive, and usable.

In Conclusion

The Aether Airlines website demonstrates how brand identity can be translated into interaction and motion. By using scroll-based structure and subtle animation, the site becomes an extension of the brand’s philosophy—intentional, calm, and human-centered. This project reflects my approach to designing digital experiences that prioritize emotion while remaining usable and scalable.
