The Challenge

How can one symbol represent the identity of Amsterdam — its culture, history, and unique character — in a way that feels authentic and timeless?

I designed a logo inspired by Amsterdam’s core elements—water, bridges, cycling, tulips, and cultural heritage—resulting in a simple, expressive mark that reflects the city’s identity.

The Solution

My Approach

Starting with the City’s Vibe

I started by looking at what makes Amsterdam feel like Amsterdam—its canals, bridges, bikes, tulips, and cultural character. The goal was to find visual elements that felt recognizable without making the logo feel too literal.

Pulling Out the Core Elements

I focused on a few key motifs—canals, bridges, tulips, and cycling—to build a stronger visual direction. Keeping the concept focused helped the final mark feel more intentional and clear.

Keeping It Simple but Expressive

I used abstract shapes to create a logo that feels clean, modern, and connected to the city. The final mark keeps things minimal while still capturing Amsterdam’s personality in a more thoughtful way.

Final Thoughts

This logo distills Amsterdam’s identity into a single, adaptable brand mark. The result balances cultural storytelling with visual clarity, demonstrating an ability to design meaningful logos that are both rooted in research and visually striking.
